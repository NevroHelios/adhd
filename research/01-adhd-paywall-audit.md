# ADHD online self-test audit (2026-10-17)

Raw subagent report, pending main-session re-verification (see 00-report.md).

Method: WebFetch of own pages/FAQ/terms plus Trustpilot/BBB. No tests taken, no forms submitted, no JS rendered, so end-of-test screens were never seen. WebSearch budget ran out near the end. Blocked fetches: HealthCentral/Psycom, cerebral.com, testlibrary.com (403); adhdtest.ai (522); CHADD (refused); Truity, Healthline/MNT quiz URLs (404).

## Bottom line
- Generic searches ("free adult adhd test", "adhd quiz") returned ~18 sites; **none** paywalled results.
- Paywall/subscription operators are a separate cluster, found by searching complaints: $1-2 "results fee" quietly converting to a $20-60 subscription, almost always with an unnamed proprietary instrument. A BBB complainant says TestLibrary appears when you Google "ADHD test" (probably via ads; not measured).
- Among legitimate sites, the common catch is an **email gate** or a **telehealth funnel**, not a paywall.

## Shares
- Generic search (~18): ~0% c/e; ~30-40% a; ~60-70% b (a quarter of those email-gated).
- Full enriched sample (~38): ~11 a, 20 b, 3-4 c, 6 e, 0 d. The c/e share is inflated by sampling.
- Legit sites use the ASRS (6/18). Paywall/subscription sites use unnamed proprietary tests and are the long ones (100 Qs, 15-40 min).

## Dark patterns
- $1-2 results fee converting to a subscription (adhdtestcenter terms, multitests, TestLibrary, personality.cc, nordicalab).
- Price revealed only after the work (adhdassessment.org, mind-test.org).
- Cross-domain operators (adhdtestcenter ↔ adhdexam; NeuraMetric clones), unverified.
- Fake social proof claims; anchor discounts (ADHDROOM).
- Email gates on legit sites (ADDA, ADHD Centre, ADHDAdvisor, Exceptional Individuals, ADHD UK, SpicyBrain); phone gate at Done.
- Cerebral's ad-analytics data consent.

## Agent's build implications
Free ASRS with the score shown already exists (MHA, Embrace Autism, adhdtests.net, freeadhdtest.org, Psychology-Tools). More defensible gaps: no email/phone gate, local-only processing, transparent scoring with citations, multi-condition, and outranking subscription funnels (a distribution problem).

## Main-session re-verification (2026-10-17)
- adhdtestcenter.org/terms (WebFetch): CONFIRMED verbatim: "48-hour trial period that costs $2.00 USD", "automatically convert to a monthly subscription", "between $19.00 and $39.00 USD per month".
- multitests.org/adhd/start and /autism/start (curl): CONFIRMED: "To view your results you'll need to start a subscription: €1.25 for a 3-day trial, then €49.95 every 28 days. It renews automatically unless cancelled." Disconfirming nuance: this text sits on the *start* page (before any question), so it's disclosed upfront, if in fine print. "Hidden until after" is not accurate for this site.
- personality.cc (curl): CONFIRMED FAQ: "You can get the results of this test through a 24-hour trial or subscription."
- BBB Scam Tracker 1281743 (WebFetch): CONFIRMED May 6 2026 TestLibrary complaint: $1.99, then "$39.99 every month", described as "bait and switch". Correction: this complaint says it was reached **via Facebook**, not Google; the "Google 'ADHD test'" quote must come from the other (March) complaint, not re-checked.
- FTC v. Genesis Tech, 17 Jun 2026 (WebFetch ftc.gov): CONFIRMED; Wisey is an "ADHD/productivity self-help course"; tactics: "free or available for a low, one-time cost", "obscure references to auto-renewing subscriptions", "make cancellation difficult".
- adhdassessment.org: curl returned no pricing text (JS-rendered), so the claim stays at user-report level.
